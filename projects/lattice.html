lattice

An automatic differentiation engine, built from the ground up to understand how gradients flow.

Python · C++17

What it does

Builds reverse-mode automatic differentiation from scalar values through tensors and neural-network components.

Implementation

Includes SGD and Adam optimizers and an MNIST multilayer perceptron example. The tensor core is being ported to C++17 with shared-storage views.

Testing

Gradients are checked against finite differences and PyTorch.
